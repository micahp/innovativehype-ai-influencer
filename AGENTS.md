# Human-owned creative decisions

Before any research, persona, content strategy, creative direction, media generation or publishing work, read `research/reference-pack-2026-10-31/README.md`, `assignments.json`, and the linked evidence files.

## Strict rules
1. **No discretionary creative substitutions.** Only use the six locked assignment IDs M-01…M-03 and S-01…S-03. Every content brief must provide an explicit owner-selected assignment ID. If absent or unclear, stop and request a decision. Do not automatically pick one.
2. **No self-initiated research/creative changes.** Do not add, rank, replace or broaden source creators/posts/photos; modify character face/body/hair/name/voice; choose a new creative aesthetic; train a model; or change cadence without a specific owner instruction and subsequent review.
3. Local SVGs are original composition **illustrations**, not canonical face images. Never use a real influencer's appearance or image as a character reference or LoRA input without separately verified rights and specific owner authorization.
4. All briefs must record assignment ID, source post/creator IDs, evidence URLs, persona version, and intended shot before generation. Reject missing, unverifiable, or fabricated metadata and metrics.
5. Research may produce *proposals* in a separate review queue. Proposals never become approved creative direction, scheduled jobs, or posts automatically.
6. No live posting, automated replies, or new spending. Leave publishing paused, media generation draft-only, and production actions gated by human approval until explicitly authorized.
7. Never imply these fictional AI creators are real people, played a game, attended an event, used a product, or received a sponsorship. Maintain AI-creator disclosure.

## Completion criteria
Code changes must include tests that reject unknown assignment IDs, inconsistent persona assignments, unapproved source swaps, or generation without reviewed canon. Do not mark the photos as downloaded or character packs as ready when they are not.
